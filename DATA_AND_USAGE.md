## Publication boundary

Published: attributed source CSV, original readable TWB, selected sanitized Prep
logic, source fingerprints, saved visual snapshots and aggregate validation.

Private: original course PDF, downloaded ZIP/TWBX/TFL containers, Hyper extract,
local source/output paths, course-account identifiers, grades and feedback.
The public TWB references a relative Hyper location but does not embed the extract.

## Interpretation

This is a historical educational case study. Anonymous survey submissions are not
representative labor-market sampling, contemporary salary advice or live vacancy
data. Currency conversion does not adjust for purchasing power or living costs.
